# VS-Bench step 2 report: camera motion

Generated by `tools/step2_report.py` from the Step 2 gates and `results/step2_axes.json`. Machine: 13th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i5-1335U, 12 threads, Linux-6.8.0-138-generic-x86_64-with-glibc2.35, Python 3.11.16, NumPy 2.4.6, MuJoCo 3.13.0. Rendered gates on `mujoco-egl` (llvmpipe (LLVM 15.0.7, 256 bits)); `VSBENCH_SOFTWARE_GL=1`, so no GPU was used.

The motion layer: `vsbench/se3.py` (closed-form exp and log on SE(3), series below 1e-6 rad), `vsbench/motion.py` (`FreeCamera` right-multiplies `exp_se3(twist * dt)` and re-orthonormalises by SVD; `clamp_twist` scales the linear and angular parts separately), `vsbench/interaction.py` (point interaction matrix, linearised and exact pixel flow) and `vsbench/trajectory.py` (one compressed `.npz` per flight, bit-identical replay). None of them imports a renderer; the MuJoCo backend and the `Renderer` interface are unchanged.

## 4. Notes

- **Analytic flow gate on the median.** The linearisation error of a point is second order in its own flow. Scaling a forward motion (`vz`) to a 1 px median moves a point at 0.3 m near the image edge by about 7 px, and its error is then 0.1 px by geometry. The gate therefore holds the median to the brief's 0.01 px, checks the fourfold shrink on both the median and the maximum, and bounds the maximum at 0.2 px. Every other axis has a maximum below 0.005 px.
- **Rendered flow.** The median endpoint error is largest for `vz` and `wz` (0.11 to 0.12 px against 0.02 to 0.04 px for the others): expansion and rotation change the appearance inside the 21 px LK window, the pure shifts do not. All of it is well inside the 0.3 px rule.
- **Replay.** `FreeCamera.step` and `Trajectory.replay` call the same `integrate` function with the logged applied twist, so a replay repeats the same floating-point operations and the poses come back bit-identical. The clamp is not re-applied on replay: re-clamping an already clamped twist can move it by one ulp.
- **Log layout.** Row `k` of a trajectory holds the pose at `t[k]`, the command that arrived there and the twist actually applied; the pose after the last row is `final_pose()`. Step 3 adds its errors and matches to the same `.npz`.
- **Step 1 gates.** Unchanged and green: the renderer, the `Renderer` interface and the backend were not touched.
