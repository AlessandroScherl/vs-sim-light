## 3. Notes

- **Depth forms.** `none` gets no depth at all, `scalar` gets the median target depth once as the distance prior, `dense` gets the depth image of the target and of every current view. `sift_ibvs` and `orb_ibvs` default to `dense` and read the depth at each inlier keypoint.
- **Stop rule.** The pose is checked at the start of every step; `converged` means `hold_steps` consecutive checks inside `stop_tol`. Stopping on the true pose is a test-only rule that Step 7 replaces.
- **Zero at target is exact.** The renderer is deterministic, so at the target the current and desired images are identical, every keypoint matches itself with zero distance, and the feature error is exactly zero; the oracle takes the logarithm of an identity, also exactly zero.
- **Negative control.** A flipped axis still passes zero-at-target and hold-at-target, because a zero twist has no sign; the restoring check is the one that catches it, on exactly that axis. It runs with a 300-step budget instead of 3000 because the flipped axis never converges.
- **Log.** `uint16` pixel coordinates times 32 store matches to 1/32 px up to 2047 px (measured worst quantisation 1/64 px); more than 500 matches per step are thinned evenly so inliers and outliers keep their mix. Frame hashes are 64-bit BLAKE2b of the raw RGB bytes.
- **Replay.** Re-rendering the logged poses in the same GL variant gives identical hashes; a different variant is reported as not identical and the video is still written, marked in red.
- **Steps 1 and 2.** Unchanged and green.
